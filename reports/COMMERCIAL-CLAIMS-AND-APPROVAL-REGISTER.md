## 1. Executive Policy Statement

In commercial enterprise contracting, publishing unapproved pricing figures, unconditional replacement warranties, or rigid turnaround SLAs creates binding legal liability, pricing inflexibility, and cash-flow risks.

This register audits all 10 key commercial claims identified in recent implementation sprints. In accordance with corporate governance directives, **all public customer-facing copy has been updated to frame commercial figures as customized quotation-based frameworks**, requiring explicit bilateral execution in a Master Service Agreement (MSA) or Statement of Work (SOW).
