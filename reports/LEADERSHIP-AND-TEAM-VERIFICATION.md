## 1. Executive Summary & Governance Policy

A credible enterprise technology and workforce solutions company must be anchored by real people, real leadership, and verified organizational governance. 

### Mandatory Governance Rules:
1. **Zero Synthetic People:** No generative AI faces, synthetic biographies, or fabricated educational credentials may be published.
2. **Zero Unrelated Stock Deception:** Stock model photos may never be displayed as named TalentXcel executives, managers, or employees.
3. **Transparent Status Staging:** Where leadership profiles, biographies, or photographs are undergoing formal corporate verification, they are explicitly marked as `REQUIRES BUSINESS OWNER VERIFICATION` or displayed with dignified corporate monograms and structured organizational mandates.
4. **Direct Business Owner Sign-Off:** Publication of named individuals requires formal written authorization from the company directors.
