## 4. Backlink Audit & Quality Verification Protocol

All backlinks referencing TalentXcel domains are evaluated against our 5-point verification criteria:

| Evaluation Metric | Acceptable Quality Threshold | Action on Non-Compliance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Domain Topical Relevance** | Must relate to Technology, IT, HR, Education, Business, or Law. | Reject/Disavow spam, gambling, or unrelated sites. |
| **Editorial Justification** | Must be embedded within genuine body copy by a human editor. | Disavow automated directory blasts or footer links. |
| **Indexing Status** | Linking page must be indexed and crawlable by Google. | Exclude non-indexed links from authority reporting. |
| **Follow vs. NoFollow** | Natural balance: ~60% Follow, ~40% NoFollow/UGC/Sponsored. | Maintain organic ratio; never force follow attributes. |
| **Anchor Text Distribution** | ~70% Brand/URL anchors, ~20% Topical, ~10% Generic. | Disavow exact-match keyword anchor spam. |
