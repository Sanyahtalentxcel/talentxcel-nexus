## 1. Executive Directive: Repeatable Institutional Referral Engines

Rather than relying purely on transactional search clicks, TalentXcel builds **repeatable bilateral partnerships** that generate compounding referral traffic, high-trust digital citations, and qualified enterprise opportunities.

Every bilateral partnership operates under five strict governance rules:
1. **Verified Organization:** Must be an accredited university, incorporated company, or recognized industry body.
2. **Authorized Signatory:** Execution requires formal sign-off by a Dean, Director, CXO, or Head of Procurement.
3. **Written Permission:** Institutional crests, trademarks, and case study details are published only after mutual written consent.
4. **Contextual Referral Tracking:** Every partner asset carries dedicated UTM tags (`utm_source=partner_name&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=partner_track`).
5. **Clear Value Exchange:** Defined mutual benefit (e.g., higher placement percentages for colleges, guaranteed talent SLAs for employers).
