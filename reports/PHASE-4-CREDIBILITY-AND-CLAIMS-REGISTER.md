## 3. Governance Protocol for New Claims

No new metric, case study, partnership logo, or leadership profile may be published to `talentxcel.co.in` unless:
1. **Documentary Proof Exists:** Signed client contract, public press release, verified analytics screenshot, or RoC statutory filing.
2. **Written Authorization:** Explicit business owner sign-off recorded in this register.
3. **Factual Accuracy:** Dates, scopes, and descriptions represent active, demonstrable realities.
